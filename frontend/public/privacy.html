Privacy policy

Last updated: 2 October 2026

CrimeSpot helps communities report crime, see where incidents cluster, share their location with people they trust, and raise emergency alerts. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have. We process personal information in line with South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA).

Who is responsible

CrimeSpot is operated by Kutlwano Mokubetsi, the responsible party under POPIA. Contact: kutlwanomokubetsi@gmail.com or 073 870 3986 (calls and WhatsApp).

What we collect

  • Account details: your email address and name. If you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address and whether Google has verified it. We do not receive your Google password or access your Google data.
  • Phone number (optional): if you add one, only your accepted friends can see it, so they can call you during an emergency alert.
  • Incident reports: the type of incident, your description, an optional place name, the map location and the time. Other users see the report but never who made it.
  • Live location (only when you choose): while you share your location or have an active emergency alert, your device sends its current position. We keep only your latest position and delete it when sharing ends. We do not keep a history of where you have been.
  • Emergency alerts: when you press SOS we store the alert, its time, your optional message and your position at that moment, so your friends can find you.
  • Friends: who you are friends with, and pending friend requests.
  • Notification details: if you turn on notifications, a technical address for your device that lets us send it alerts.

How we use it

  • To run your account and sign you in securely.
  • To show incident reports and detect crime hotspots on the map.
  • To let you share your location with friends you choose, and to alert them in an emergency.
  • To moderate reports and prevent abuse, for example by limiting how many reports one account can send.

We do not sell your information, show advertising, or use your data for marketing.

Who can see what

  • Everyone signed in: incident reports, without the reporter's identity, and hotspots.
  • Your accepted friends: your name, your phone number if you added one, your location while you share it with them, and your emergency alerts.
  • Moderators: reports waiting for review, to verify or reject them.

Where it is stored

CrimeSpot runs on Render (hosting) and Neon (database), with servers in Frankfurt, Germany. Sign-in is handled by our own Keycloak server on the same hosting. Push notifications pass through your browser's notification service (for example Google or Apple). Germany is covered by the EU General Data Protection Regulation, which gives protection comparable to POPIA.

How long we keep it

  • Live location: deleted when you stop sharing, when the time you chose runs out, or when you end an emergency alert.
  • Incident reports and alerts: kept while your account exists, so hotspots and alert history stay accurate. Hotspots only use reports from the past 30 days.
  • Account data: until you ask us to delete your account.

Your rights

Under POPIA you may ask to see the personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, and object to how we process it. You can update your name and phone number on the Profile page, stop location sharing at any time, remove friends, and turn notifications off. To get a copy of your data or delete your account, email kutlwanomokubetsi@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days.

If you are unhappy with how we handle your information, you may complain to the Information Regulator of South Africa: inforegulator.org.za.

Security

Connections are encrypted, passwords are handled by Keycloak and never stored by the CrimeSpot app itself, and access to data is limited by role. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong, unique password.

Children

CrimeSpot is not intended for children under 18.

Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will update the date above and tell you in the app.
